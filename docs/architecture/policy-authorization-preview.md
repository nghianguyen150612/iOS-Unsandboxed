# Policy authorization preview

`manager.sh policy preview /opt/ios-unsandboxed-scopes/example/program` is local and read-only. It never executes, signs, rewrites or sends the target to the daemon. P010 records descriptor-backed identity, SHA-256, ARM64 Mach-O type and signature-command classification and revalidates identity. P003 is evaluated once with the operational configuration disabled and once with a temporary simulated enabled/verified context to test scope matching. That temporary context is not persisted or returned as permission. Results include NOT_CONFIGURED, OUTSIDE_ALLOWED_SCOPE, UNSAFE_DIRECTORY, UNSAFE_TARGET, IDENTITY_MISMATCH, UNSUPPORTED_MACHO, MISSING_SIGNATURE_COMMAND, PREPARATION_NOT_IMPLEMENTED or HYPOTHETICALLY_ELIGIBLE as applicable. All results also report BACKEND_UNVERIFIED, missing prerequisites, runtime verification false, and operational authorization false.

The P009 audited source revision remains `61ce86d7d3f00e5395f4d42bebda0e5dce04946a`. Source provenance is not installed binary identity; component presence is not live hook evidence; a digest without a trusted reference is not a trusted hash match. A parsed or ad-hoc signature is not proof of kernel acceptance, fully unsigned execution, or launch capability. No private Amethyst XPC, F_ADDSIGS, trust cache, kernel, sandbox or launch operation is used. Even a stable P010 revalidation is a point-in-time observation and cannot bind a later pathname-based launch.

P012 needs a separately reviewed, versioned decision and launch-boundary contract that binds policy and current identity to the actual launch request. It also needs authoritative installed-build and hook evidence, runtime verification, activation and recovery design, and physical-device gates. P011 does not begin that work.
